## Resolved design decisions
- A malformed handoff must fail loudly (clear error in the log and finish DM) and leave the worktree in a state that can be resumed or re-run. It must not silently accept the bad file, and it must not leave finished work with no path forward.
- Validate the handoff shape early, before any step that is hard to undo.
- Do not loosen what counts as a valid handoff to make the stranding go away.

## Scope
